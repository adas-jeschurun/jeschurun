About Jeschurun, its purpose, and its editorial direction.

Jeschurun is a multilingual publication devoted to the teachings of Rabbi Samson Raphael Hirsch and those who continue his path, with Torah, halakhic, historical, and intellectual writing in the spirit of Torah im Derech Eretz.

Published by Levi Jöel Rits · Haifa
