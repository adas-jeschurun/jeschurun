Ki Tetze

The Other Side of Courage

What makes a person moral — inner conviction, or a system of external restraints? In ordinary life the distinction is difficult to test. War changes the conditions: a person receives a weapon and the authority to use force, while many of the ordinary restraints of civilian life grow weaker.
