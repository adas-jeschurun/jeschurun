# About Us

**Jeschurun** is a journal devoted to Torah study, published as part of the **Adath Jeschurun (עדת ישורון)** initiative. It is dedicated to the study and dissemination of the legacy of Rabbi Samson Raphael Hirsch, of blessed memory, his thought, and his educational approach, founded on the principle of **Torah im Derech Eretz (תורה עם דרך ארץ)**.

The journal's issues and articles are prepared, among other sources, on the basis of lessons given by our teacher, Rabbi Levi Yoel Rits (shlita), on Rabbi Hirsch's legacy and teachings, Jewish thought, the Talmud, and Halacha.

Alongside these articles, we publish translations of works by rabbis and thinkers who continued to develop Rabbi Hirsch's approach, as well as historical and biographical articles about individuals and movements that have left their mark on Torah scholarship, Jewish education, and thought. Our materials are published in several languages, with the aim of making the rich heritage of Jewish thought accessible to a broad contemporary readership.

Questions on Halacha and Jewish thought may also be submitted through our **“Ask the Rabbi”** section. With God's help, and *bli neder* (without undertaking a binding commitment), we will endeavour to respond whenever possible.

We pray for the full and speedy recovery of our teacher, together with all the sick among the people of Israel. May he merit to continue teaching Torah and educating students for many good years to come.
